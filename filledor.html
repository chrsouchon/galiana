IV LA FILLE D'OR

La fille d'or aux berges du printemps chante
Et mai répond. Joli moi de mai fleuri,
Ne connais-tu le noir soleil qui me hante,
L'oiseau mauvais dont ne se taira le cri?

Presque à mi-voix rêve la fille sauvage
Dans la pénombre où s'obstine la chanson.
Août comme un fer posera la grille sage.
Le parc fermé refermera l'échanson.

La fille brune en sa boutique. Ténèbres.
La guêpe tourne où flotte l'odeur du pain.
Applaudissez mes cantilènes funèbres,
Le baladin en costume de sapin.

Michel Galiana (c) 1991

And May answers. Lovely, blossoming month of May,
Don't you feel the dark sun that within me abides,
This bird of ill omen whose hooting never fades?

In the low pitch wherein they stubbornly linger.
August will close on her its demure gate and chains.
Once shut the park will lock away the cupbearer.

Brown maiden in your shop, tenebrous and dismal.
A wasp circles around where scents of bread still float.
O please, applause my songs: they fit a funeral,
And the minstrel dressed in his wooden overcoat!

This seasonal descent mirrors Nerval’s emotional descent.
Michel is saying:
“Your black sun is not eternal; it has seasons.”
This is a correction of Nerval’s fatalism.

VII. The Final Gesture: applause for the funeral song
Nerval ends El Desdichado with a gesture of ambiguous triumph:
"Et j’ai deux fois vainqueur traversé l’Achéron…"
Michel ends with:
"Applaudissez mes cantilènes funèbres…"
This is a Nervalian inversion:
• Nerval: triumph over death
• Michel: acceptance of death
• Nerval: heroic crossing
• Michel: theatrical funeral
• Nerval: mythic grandeur
• Michel: ironic resignation
Michel is not imitating Nerval; he is answering him.

VIII. Synthesis: How the two poems speak to each other
El Desdichado
• The poet is haunted by the black sun.
• The beloved is lost.
• The cry is internal.
• The descent is mythic.
• The ending is ambiguous triumph.
La fille d’or
• The poet shows the black sun to May.
• The beloveds are threefold.
• The cry is external (the bird).
• The descent is seasonal and biographical.
• The ending is ironic funeral.
Michel’s poem is a dialogue, a correction, and a continuation of Nerval’s masterpiece.
It is Nerval rewritten through:
• modern tragedy,
• personal memory,
• seasonal time,
• and a triadic feminine archetype.
